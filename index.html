This Privacy Notice for Denary describes how and why we might access, collect, store, use, and share your personal information when you use our services, including when you download and use our mobile application Denary. Questions or concerns? Contact us at denaryapp@gmail.com.

1. What Information Do We Collect?

Personal information you provide to us

Denary does not require an account, login, email address, or phone number. The only information you provide is a display name that you choose yourself when you join a game — we do not collect your real name.

Sensitive Information. We do not process sensitive information.

Payments. Denary is completely free to play. It contains no in-app purchases and processes no payment data of any kind.

Information automatically collected

When you use the Services we automatically collect an anonymous device identifier, used only to let you rejoin a game session after a brief disconnection. This identifier is not linked to your display name or any other personal information. The app does not use any analytics or advertising SDKs.

2. How Do We Process Your Information?

We process your information to provide, improve, and administer our Services, communicate with you, for security and fraud prevention, and to comply with law. Specifically:

  • To deliver services to the user — we use your display name and an anonymous device identifier to run the game, track scores, and display the leaderboard for the duration of a session.
  • To enable user-to-user communications — in online multiplayer, your display name is visible to other players in the same room.
  • To save or protect an individual's vital interest — we may process your information when necessary to prevent harm.

3. What Legal Bases Do We Rely On?

If you are located in the EU or UK: We rely on the following legal bases under GDPR: Consent, Performance of a Contract, Legal Obligations, and Vital Interests.

If you are located in Canada: We may process your information with your express or implied consent, or in exceptional circumstances permitted by law without consent.

4. When and With Whom Do We Share Your Information?

We may share information with third-party service providers who perform services on our behalf:

  • Multiplayer Infrastructure: Firebase Realtime Database (Google) — powers live online game rooms
  • Anonymous Sign-In: Firebase Authentication (Google) — issues an anonymous device identifier so you can rejoin a room after a brief disconnection

We do not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Denary has no in-app purchases, so we do not use or share information with any payment processor.

We may also share information in connection with business transfers (mergers, acquisitions), and other users will see your chosen display name within a shared game session.

We have not sold or shared any personal information to third parties for a business or commercial purpose in the preceding twelve months.

5. Do We Use Cookies and Tracking Technologies?

No. Denary does not use browser cookies, analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or any other tracking technology. The only data automatically collected is the anonymous device identifier described in Section 1, which is used solely to let you rejoin a game session.

6. How Long Do We Keep Your Information?

Your display name is not linked to any account or persistent identity — you choose it fresh each time you join a game. Multiplayer room data (room codes, scores, and the secret number for that round) is stored in Firebase Realtime Database and is used only to run that game session. The anonymous device identifier described in Section 1 is retained only for as long as needed to let you rejoin a game after a brief disconnection. We keep this information only for as long as necessary to operate the Services, and no longer.

7. How Do We Keep Your Information Safe?

We have implemented appropriate technical and organisational security measures designed to protect the security of any personal information we process. However, no electronic transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be 100% secure. You should only access the Services within a secure environment.

8. Do We Collect Information from Minors?

We do not knowingly collect, solicit data from, or market to children under 13 years of age. By using the Services, you represent that you are at least 13, or that you are the parent or guardian of a minor and consent to their use of the Services. If you become aware of any data we may have collected from children under 13, please contact us at denaryapp@gmail.com.

Depending on where you are located, you may have rights including: access to your personal information, rectification or erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and the right to object to processing.
